# 🏠 Manzilo

**Modern short-term rental platform in Tajikistan 🇹🇯**

---

## 🚀 About

**Manzilo.tj** is a proptech platform transforming the **rental market in Tajikistan**.  
We connect property owners with tenants through a seamless, secure, and scalable digital experience.

Our platform simplifies how users **discover, book, and manage rental properties**, bringing transparency and efficiency to a traditionally fragmented market.

---

## 🌍 Vision

To become the **leading digital rental ecosystem in Tajikistan**, and expand across Central Asia.

---

## 🎯 Mission

- Digitize the rental market in Tajikistan  
- Empower property owners to monetize efficiently  
- Provide users with verified and high-quality listings  
- Deliver fast, reliable, and secure booking experience  

---

## 🧩 Platform Architecture

Our system is built using a **modular and scalable architecture**:

- 🔐 Identity & Authentication Service  
- 🏠 Listing Management Service  
- 📅 Booking & Reservation Service  
- 💳 Payment & Billing Service  
- 📊 Analytics & Reporting  
- 🔔 Notification Service
